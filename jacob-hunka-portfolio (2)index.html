The Human Behind the Résumé

I'm the person friends call before buying a car. I explain trim levels, walk through negotiation strategy, and make sure they feel confident — not sold to. I've done this informally for years. The Product Guide role is simply that, made professional.

My career has always orbited one core belief: people perform best when they feel safe, seen, and genuinely supported. I've led teams of 31, managed a busy restaurant floor at midnight, and taught English to students in Taipei who didn't yet speak my language. Every role demanded the same skill: reading the room and meeting people where they are.

I'm at a point in life where I can chase passion over paycheck. Rivian is that passion. I've believed in this company since I took delivery of one of the first 7,000 R1Ts. I'm not arriving to learn about the mission — I've been living it.

I'm kind. I work hard. I'm endlessly curious — an autodidact who reads widely, travels eagerly, and finds genuine joy in learning something new from the person in front of me.

Why Rivian

🌿
Mission Alignment

RJ Scaringe has been clear about what Rivian is trying to solve — that vehicles sit at the center of so many of the planet's challenges, and that tension between loving the outdoors and contributing to its degradation is what drove him to start the company. I connect with that premise deeply. Rivian's focus on reducing the impact of getting there — without sacrificing capability — aligns with how I think about adventure, responsibility, and building long-term customer trust.

⚡
The Living Vehicle

A vehicle that improves over time through software is genuinely thrilling. The auto industry is becoming a software industry — and I've been tracking that convergence with real enthusiasm, not just professional interest.

☀️
Sun-Powered Living

My home has solar. My truck runs on it. When I tell a customer that their vehicle can be charged by sunlight, I'm not reciting a feature — I'm sharing something I experience every single day.

🤝
People-First Culture

Rivian's commitment to diversity and being genuinely good people isn't a talking point to me — it's a filter. Creating environments where everyone feels welcome and can be themselves has been central to how I've led every team.

🚙
Actual Ownership

I'm not a candidate who researched the brand for an interview. I'm one of the first 7,000 R1T owners. I have an R2 reservation. I know the product from the inside — the excitement, the quirks, the loyalty it builds.

🔭
The Long View

A lifetime of reading Clarke and Asimov has given me a genuine appreciation for the long arc of technology. Electrification isn't a trend — it's the direction. I want to be part of the team helping people step into that future with confidence.
